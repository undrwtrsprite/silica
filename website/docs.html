01 / Setup

Getting started

Up and running on Apple Silicon in under a minute.

Requirements

  • Mac with Apple Silicon (M1 or newer)
  • macOS 14 Sonoma or later
  • A Microsoft account that owns Minecraft: Java Edition
  • About 17 MB for the launcher app

First launch

  1. Open Silica-0.1.11.dmg and drag Silica into Applications.
  2. Sign in with Microsoft — Silica verifies ownership and fetches the clean Beta 1.7.3 client jar from Mojang.
  3. Click Play. Silica prepares a patched copy at launch and leaves your original jar untouched.
02 / Graphics

Render distance & distant LODs

Silica replaces the 4 vanilla presets with a 2–32 chunk slider and optional real-block LODs beyond that.

LOD Settings

Open Options → Video Settings → LOD Settings in-game to configure distant terrain:

  • Enable Distant LODs: Off by default; toggle on for terrain past your render distance.
  • LOD Distance: Choose how many chunks of simplified real-block terrain load beyond your real chunks (up to 76 chunks total at 12 + 64).
  • Tiered detail: Near LOD rings keep higher surface detail and transition smoothly into coarser tiers further out.

Vulkan & Metal

Silica translates Beta 1.7.3's fixed-function rendering onto Vulkan over MoltenVK/Metal instead of legacy macOS OpenGL.

  • No Rosetta 2 translation layer required.
  • Linear fog and far clip scale automatically with the 2–32 chunk slider.
  • If you hit thermal throttling on long runs at 32 chunks, lowering real chunks to 12–16 while keeping LODs on recovers headroom.
03 / In-game

Controls, inventory & audio

Inventory fixes

  • Sort button: Groups and compacts chest or player inventory stacks cleanly.
  • Drag spread & bulk craft: Drag stacks across slots and Shift-click to craft full stacks.
  • Number-key swaps: Hover any slot and press 1–9 to swap with your hotbar.

Controls & F3

  • Sneak toggle: Switch between hold and toggle in Controls.
  • Zoom key: Smooth FOV zoom while held.
  • Debug & cheats: Expanded F3 overlay and optional single-player commands with Tab completion.

Sound & captions

  • Separate volume sliders for categories instead of one master toggle.
  • Optional directional sound captions in the lower corner.
04 / Saves

Worlds, backups & mods

World backups & import

Silica keeps Beta 1.7.3 saves compatible with vanilla so you can move worlds freely in either direction.

  • Create timestamped zip backups directly from the launcher before testing new builds or mods.
  • Import existing Beta 1.7.3 world folders or export any Silica world back out.

Mod jar checker experimental

Point Silica at a modded Beta 1.7.3 jar and it dry-runs its patch set before launch without modifying your file.

  • Green: No conflicting class patches detected.
  • Yellow / Red: Overlaps with Silica patches or calls unmapped OpenGL functions.
05 / Help

Troubleshooting

macOS Gatekeeper warning

Silica is ad-hoc signed rather than notarized with an Apple Developer ID. If macOS says the app cannot be opened:

xattr -cr /Applications/Silica.app

Or right-click Silica.app → Open once.
